I built a tool this week: PMOS, a Product Manager Operating System generator.

The problem: every PM works differently. Some live in PRDs, some barely write documents and spend all day in execution. Most AI prompts and templates treat every PM the same.

PMOS asks you to configure your own working style (discovery, strategy, documents, metrics, execution, communication, GTM, each with a depth: skip, light, or heavy), your tools, and the daily habits you want an assistant to follow. It generates one markdown file. You paste it into Claude, ChatGPT, or whatever you use, and it behaves like it actually knows how you work.

Everything runs in the browser. No account, no backend, no data collected.
